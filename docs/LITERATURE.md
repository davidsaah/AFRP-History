# The library

The book is built on a working library of roughly **190 items** — about 2 GB of
scanned scholarship, archival facsimiles, censuses and family histories.

**The files are not in this repository, and will not be.** Most are not ours to
redistribute: published scholarship still in copyright, archival scans belonging to
their holding institutions, and family histories whose rights sit with their authors'
heirs. `docs/SOURCES.md` sets out the rights position; this file is the *index*, so a
reader can see exactly what the book was built on and go find any item themselves.

Every filename below is the one used in the book's citations. Where a note in the book
prints a filename in `code`, it is a file on this shelf.

---

## Naming convention

`YYYY_Author_Short_Title_Series.pdf` — year read off the item's own title page, never
guessed. `19xx` / `20xx` marks an item whose year could not be established from the
work itself. `EXCERPT` marks a partial scan; `FULLSCAN` a high-resolution complete one
kept alongside a smaller working copy.

---

## Provenance flags

The book distinguishes **what is on the shelf** from **what is properly citable**, and
the shelf record says where a file came from.

| flag | meaning |
|---|---|
| **OA** | open access — freely and legally downloadable from the publisher |
| **z-lib** | obtained from a z-library mirror; the text is sound but the copy's provenance is not clean. **Five items.** Cite the published edition, not the file. |
| **inst.** | institutional scan (IRCICA, TKGM, HathiTrust, archive.org) |

The five **z-lib** items are the Shaheen *Pictorial History*, both Zahran volumes,
McDowell's *Notes from Ramallah 1939*, and Barghouti's *I Saw Ramallah*. Each is in
print or has a published edition; the book cites the edition.

## Known gaps

Named so they are visible rather than papered over.

- **Shahîd, *Byzantium and the Arabs in the Sixth Century* (BASIC)**, 2 vols in 4 — not obtainable;
  the v57 claim about his source base was withdrawn as unverified for exactly this reason.
- **Musil, *Arabia Petraea* III** — read online, no local copy filed.
- **Hütteroth & Abdulfattah p. 171** — the Karak figures are confirmed by an independent
  arithmetic route but nobody on this project has seen the page.
- **Eight `palestine-studies.org` PDFs** — the host blocks automated fetching; they need a browser.
- **al-Furqān, *Liwāʾ al-Quds al-Sharīf*, 6 vols** — to purchase; vol. 5 = TD 516/1562, vol. 6 = TD 515/1596.
- **Jubeh & Bshara**, *Ramallah: Architecture and History*; **Riwaq Registry** vol. 2;
  **Amiry & Anani**, *Throne Village Architecture* — to purchase.
- The two Arabic Ḥaddādīn clan histories (1991 and the later Amman volume) — neither held.
