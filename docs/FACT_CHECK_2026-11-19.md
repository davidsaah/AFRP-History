# Fact check, 19 November 2026

David asked me to "check all the facts and figures to make sure everything is accurate and consistent", and to "look at the narrative in the movie to see if there is anything missing in the book".

**How:**
- I read the whole book in six parts (front matter to chapter 13, plus the appendices), including the text of every figure and all 524 notes.
- I checked arithmetic, dates, figure labels against the text, and notes against the sentences that cite them.
- I checked quoted figures against the text copies of the Literature shelf (`Family Tree\Literature\_Text`).
- A seventh pass compared every fact that recurs across chapters.

Per David's standing instruction, small source differences (rounding, spellings, off-by-a-few totals) were left alone. I verified each fix below against the source or the book's own other passages before making it.

## Fixed in this change

### Arithmetic and numbers
- **Figure 10.10:** the 1905 and 1922 counts are seventeen years apart, not sixteen. The credit now names Shāhīn's 1905 count and Barron (1923), not Schick (1896).
- **Oldest-ground map:** distances are now computed from the dots, as the note on distances says every label is. ʿAin Samiya's dot moved to the coordinates in its own note. Shuqba now reads 17.7 km, not 19.3, and ʿAin Samiya 15.6 km, not 13.8.
- **Tell en-Naṣba:** the chapter 1 text said "about two and a half kilometres". It now says about two, matching the Iron Age map (1.8 km).
- **Shunem** is some eighty kilometres north, not sixty.
- **Ḥaddādīn sons as fathers in 1562:** the count is "Jiryis 2" (Sālim and Dīb, Ḥammūdeh p. 46), so thirteen households out of sixty-three, not twelve. Chapter 8 already named both.
- **Figure 7.1 and the chapter 7 text:** they now say 19 of the 36 Kasābra households came up, with 44 more Christian households (63 in all). Before, they said "the 36 … with 27 further", which the chapter itself later corrects.
- **The Shāhīn test harness:** the tally now matches its own table: 12 confirmed, 15 new, 5 refined, 3 corrected and 1 in conflict. It now speaks of eight clans, not nine.
- **J-Z640:** the marker is about three thousand years older than the Jafnids, not two and a half thousand.
- **Ibn ʿInaba (d. 1424):** he wrote six centuries before the family went looking, not four.
- **"Six searches"** (four searches and two controls), not five.
- **Figure 0.1:** four of the twelve chapters open with "no evidence", not four of "nine eras".

### Dates
- **Josephus BJ IV.551:** AD 69, not 68, as in the text and in note 70.
- **1562 is when the families were counted.** Arrival was by 1561, when they were in court as people of Ramallah, so chapter 4 now says "are counted on the ridge in 1562". Chapter 9 now says the families came up from Bayt Jālā "in the 1550s".
- **Jordan:** citizenship from December 1949 and annexation in April 1950. The chapter's own note had this, and the text didn't.
- **Camps:** al-Amʿarī, al-Jalazūn and Qalandiyā were set up in 1949, as the table and map say. The "What was happening" box had July 1948.
- **Suez:** Israel invaded Sinai on 29 October, in concert with Britain and France. The two allies attacked from 31 October.
- **Birzeit University:** closed from 10 January 1988, after the contemporary JPS chronology. Two passages said 8 January and one said 10.
- **Muṣṭafā al-Tamīmī** was hit on 9 December 2011 and died the next day.
- **Jawāhir Abū Raḥma** died on 1 January 2011, not in 2010.
- **Gobat's school:** about 1848–50 in both places. One passage had 1846.
- **1912:** the council celebrated the anniversary of the Sultan's accession, not a coronation. Mehmed V came to the throne in 1909.
- **Rafeedie's novel:** written from 1994, the year of his arrest, not 1993.
- **Balfour demonstration:** now "November 1917". The 8 November date isn't in the cited source and falls a day before the Declaration was published.
- **The 1901 carriage road:** opened as far as al-Bīra (Grant), which Shāhīn calls the Nablus road. One sentence had it "finished to Nablus".

### Sources say something different
- **Taraki's Christian villages** are ʿAyn ʿArīk and al-Ṭayyiba, not Birzeit and Ṭaybeh.
- **The 1596 register:** chapters 7 and 13 now agree that Shaheen read it, that it repeats the 71 Christian names of 1562, and that only the nine Muslim names are still unread.
- **The Manāra lions:** Shaheen (2005, p. 224) names the sculptor, Salem Ghanayem of the Jirius clan. The book said the name was unknown.
- **Mūsā bin Baraka** is in both the 1553 and the 1562 lists (Ḥammūdeh p. 46).
- **Our Lady of the Annunciation, 1895:** under Fr Saba Salem Elias Mogannam (Shāhīn, twice), not Fr Yaʿqūb.
- **The Pictorial History of Ramallah (1992)** is Naseeb Shaheen's, not Azeez Shaheen's.
- **Guy de Lusignan** was not yet king on 7 March 1186, as note 135 says. Three labels and captions called him king.
- **Cadora gives two dates for Tabbāra:** 18 May in his list of governors, 21 May in his narrative.
- **Shāhīn's 13,500** is a local count of 1953. Jordan held no census that year, and chapter 10 says so.
- **Boy Scouts:** Shāhīn's 1928 (with Labīb Jiryis Ḥishmeh) is now printed beside Cadora's 1939. Shāhīn's 1910 is now printed beside Cadora's 1908 for Dr Nicola Shehadeh.
- **The camp populations** are PCBS 2017, not 2007.
- **al-Jalazūn land:** the table now matches the chapter's own account: leased from the Jordanian government, with part of the land owned by families of Jifnā.
- **Sijill 48:** the court record is p. 88 of the register. p. 54 is the printed edition's page.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - **Ophir and Havilah** are disputed, so the sentence now says what note 6 says.
  - **The Georgian kings** descend from Noah's son Japheth, not a grandson.
  - **Khirbet et-Tireh's dating** now follows al-Houdalieh.
  - **The Maʾrib sluice towers** are dated to the sixth century, the dam's first works to the eighth and seventh, as notes 47 and 50 say.
  - **The 1374 → 1800 sentence** now says "only mentions … in the court records", because chapter 8 has the 1706 Patriarchate documents.
  - **Musil's list** drops Muʾta.
  - **Gharandal** is south of Karak.
  - **Irby and Mangles** are the exception to "a fifth to two-fifths".
  - **The Friends' clinic** was founded in 1883 and run by Maʿlouf from 1891.
  - **ʿAbadān 1, not ʿInān 75,** places Ghassān in western Arabia.
  - **"Seven outsider families"**, matching chapter 8.
  - **The steadfast chapter** now hedges Ayyūb Mīkhāʾīl and Cadora's 25,000 the way chapters 10 and 11 do.
  - **The 1938 range of the dead** now reads "more than twenty to about eighty" in both places.

## Questions for David (not changed)
1. **Rāshid at generation 66.** If the generation gap is counted the way the chapter counts its father-to-son links, 931 years ÷ 30 = 31 steps puts Rāshid at 65, not 66. The appendix's "thirteen generations from Adam to ʿĀbir" pulls the other way: Genesis has fourteen. So 66 holds only if both choices are deliberate. The number runs through the whole book, so I've left it.
2. **The 1967 twin-town row:** the book has 21,808 people and 34.2% Christian. The census introduction on the shelf gives 25,171 and 29.6%. Table 1 of the Levy copy should settle it.
3. **Yāqūt on Ghassān's water:** notes 98 and 103 disagree about which placing Yāqūt credits to Ibn al-Kalbī. There's no Yāqūt text on the shelf.
4. **Where the remainder of the Ḥaddādīn settled:** the Shaheen 2005 text copy (p. 16) says el-ʿAzeeyyeh, and the book says Maʿīn. Check the printed page.
5. **Katie Gabriel's years:** the sources give 40, 41 and "over 45". The book prints all three in different places.
6. **The Shāhīn harness:** its one open conflict is the Federation (1958), but chapter 11 calls that resolved and chapter 13 names 1901 instead. Pick one.
7. **Download run of 16 August:** 43 + 5 + 2 = 50, but the total says 49.
8. **The 1936 al-Maskūbiyya episode** ("winter of 1936", hanged November 1939) may need Nayrūz's own wording.

## The film against the book
The final narration (v7) and the war additions for v8 have about 230 claims. About 214 are in the book.

**Worth adding to the book:**
- **Chapter 2:** one sentence on method. Robinson and Smith found the biblical sites by asking villagers the names of their own places. The source is already in the bibliography.

**Check the source first:**
- **Justinian's grant of kingship to al-Ḥārith:** the film says 529. Procopius gives no exact year; scholars say about 529–531.
- **B'Tselem's 72 people of Ramallah itself killed since 2000:** the v8 notes themselves say to re-run the count.

**Left out:**
- **The Yaʿrub-to-Azd rungs of John's chart:** the book treats the chart as a guide, not as evidence.
- **The family-tree figures in the closing:** they will change.

**The film disagrees with the book, and the book looks right:**
- **Shot 29:** the girls' school had twenty pupils and fifty only by 1873, across four schools. It's not "fifty within months".
- **Shot 2:** "Nobody had written his story down" is wrong. Dissard wrote it down in 1905, and the film's own shot 19 says so.
- **Shot 12:** the caption gives Ibn Durayd for "Ghassān, a spring". The water reading is Ibn al-Kalbī's and al-Hamdānī's.
- **Shot 38b:** the caption cites the 1953 town register for Abū Rayya's words. They're from his 1980 book, as the film's own shot 39 says.
- **Shot 16:** "a Crusader king" should be "Guy de Lusignan", who was crowned only later in 1186.
- **Shot 39:** "Two brothers" should be "two of the brothers". Ṣabra had three.
- **Nabī Yaʿqūb:** David's decision 8 on Ayyūb Mīkhāʾīl still stands. The book now hedges in both places.
