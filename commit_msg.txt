Chapter 10 — the void:
- GHASSAN IS STILL THERE IN 1400. The documentary void is not six hundred
  years. Ghassan is entered as a living people at al-Balqa', the Yarmuk and
  Hims in al-Qalqashandi's Qala'id al-juman (ed. Ibyari, p. 97), sourced
  through al-'Umari's Masalik al-absar to Badr al-Din Yusuf b. Zammakh
  al-Hamdani, mihmandar al-'arab — the Mamluk officer paid to know which
  tribes existed and where they camped.
- THE WORD UNDERNEATH. Al-'Umari's salība section (Masalik iv.358) and the
  mechanism of the silence. Names Wadi Bani Zayd — this district — with two
  settled lineages, 160 years before Rashid.
- AND THE MAN WHO SHOULD HAVE KNOWN. Mujir al-Din, Jerusalem 1495, printed
  at full strength against the above with its own weakness declared.
- THE TRADITION HAS ITS OWN WORD FOR THIS. Nasab al-qat', Ibn 'Inaba
  (ʿUmdat al-talib pp. 372, 161); Ibn Khaldun naming Ghassan at Muqaddima II.9.
- WHAT THE OTHER ARCHIVE CALLS THEM. Syriac Tayyaye / Beth Harith; Millar
  on the absence of "Ghassanid" or "Jafnid" inside the empire.
- FIVE SEARCHES AND WHAT THEY ACTUALLY PROVE. The Shamila name-test with
  its controls visible.
- THE INTERVAL NOBODY MEASURED FOR US. Generation interval as a stated
  assumption; J-Z640 as a claim that fails on inspection; the Palestinian
  genetics gap.

Chapter 11 — Karak:
- KARAK WAS A CHRISTIAN TOWN. 1596: 103 Christian households against 78
  Muslim. Plus Burckhardt's sentence running the family's road backwards.
- MUSIL ASKED THEM HIMSELF. Arabia Petraea III 84-85. The Karak Haddadin
  named Khirbet al-Haddad, a ruin on their own plateau. Four accounts now,
  printed as four.

Corrections to standing text:
- "Karak and Shawbak defterleri are NOT in Hutteroth and Abdulfattah" —
  they are; both are nahiyas of liwa' 'Ajlun, pp. 169-173.
- al-Hudayda: narrowed, not reversed. Wrong about existence, right about
  consequence.
- "the observed male-line generational interval" -> "an assumed" (4 places).
- documentary void extended from c. 890 to c. 1300; master timeline gains
  two rows.

Withdrawn before print:
- The Chabot footnote. Michel le Syrien searched in full: zero hits for
  "Ghassan" across all three translated volumes against 593 for Taiyaye.
- Shahid's foundations — unverified, BASIC not obtainable.
- "Mujir al-Din mentions Ghassan zero times" -> once, formulaically.
- Musil: kh. not b. — Khirbet al-Haddad, a place not a lineage.
- salība glossed backwards; corrected reading is the stronger one.
- J-Z640 is 4,600 ybp not 3,600; Nebel 2000 is Human Genetics not AJHG;
  Zalloua 2024 states no interval; Fernandes 2015 is mtDNA and is dropped
  from the male-line argument.
